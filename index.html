05 Patents

Granted US patents in electronic design automation, assigned to Synopsys, Inc.

US 12,626,039 B2 Granted · In force

Associating physical design metrics of a circuit design with register transfer level representations of the circuit design

Filed 23 Sep 2022 · Granted 12 May 2026 · Application 17/951,840 · Assignee Synopsys, Inc.

Physical design metrics — timing, congestion, power and other layout-dependent measures — are computed for sub-circuits of an integrated circuit, then mapped back to the corresponding RTL source elements (lines, modules, and user-defined constructs). Aggregating these metrics per RTL element surfaces physical-design reality directly to front-end designers.

Co-inventors B. R. Rashingkar, A. Saunders, D. Chang, J. J. Loescher, O. W. Kozber, L. Tao, S. Majumder, C. Williams

US 12,073,156 B2 Granted · In force

Propagating physical design information through logical design hierarchy of an electronic circuit

Filed 15 Mar 2022 · Granted 27 Aug 2024 · Application 17/695,712 · Publication US 2022/0300687 A1 · Assignee Synopsys, Inc.

Physical design metrics from different parts of a circuit's physical design are propagated along the logic hierarchy of the design. By relating the physical design to the logic design, metrics are attributed to individual logic blocks and presented through a user interface that displays the logic hierarchy annotated with those metrics.

Co-inventors A. Jalota, A. Saunders, A. Kanagaraj, D. Chang, E. Rajendran, P. Gupta, R. Murgai, S. Majumder, V. Chatzi, B. R. Rashingkar
